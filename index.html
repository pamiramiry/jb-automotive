When to Call a Mechanic in Mississauga

A rough guide, not a diagnosis. Tell us what you're hearing or seeing and we'll take it from there.

Car is slow to start, or the battery keeps dying

Often the battery, sometimes the charging system. Test it before replacing anything. Call and describe it.

Check engine light on, or back on after a repair

Could be minor or not. A scan tells us where to look. Call and describe it.

Squealing or grinding when you brake

Pads are often getting low. Grinding means book it now. Call and describe it.

Temperature gauge climbing

Don't drive it far. Often a coolant leak, the thermostat or a fan. Call and describe it.

Car pulls, or the steering feels loose

Often alignment, tires or a worn suspension part. Call and describe it.

New rattle or clunk over bumps

Usually a suspension or exhaust part working loose. Call and describe it.

You've got a quote and aren't sure about it

Bring the paperwork and we'll go through it with you. Call and describe it.
