# Surface: JB Automotive one-page site (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a stranger from Google search on a phone, deciding in seconds whether to call. Action: call (905) 276-2732; secondary, the quote form. Proof: real Google review words, 4.7 from 41 reviews, customers for almost 20 years. Constraints: PRODUCT.md SEO and brand commitments; keep the rolling odometer stats and the quote form; no colourway switcher, no stamp.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet luxury workshop. The page earns trust the way a high-end service house does: silence, black and white, one decisive photograph per screen, and customers' own words set large. It refuses the category default of a split hero with badges, a card grid of services, and a button pair after every section.

OWN-WORLD: True black and white with two greys, no accent colour. Every photograph in one muted colour grade (about 60% saturation, slightly warm; changed from black and white at the user's request 2026-10-27), never under text. Marcellus (flared engraved serif) for display; Hanken Grotesk for text. Hairline rules, square photos edges, 2px button corners, generous empty space; buttons are solid white on black and solid black on white.

STORY: The visitor sees a serious, calm shop; reads that JB tells the truth about the car and has saved a customer from dealer upsells more than once; believes it from the reviews and the 20-year regulars; calls John.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim bar with the wordmark left and a Call link right. A full-width 2.39:1 muted-colour engine-bay photo with light grain, capped so the Call button stays in the first screen; it opens like a film (letterbox, push-in, headline words rising), and on desktop a muted graded video loop of hands at work fades in behind it with a visible pause button. Below on black: "Auto Repair in Mississauga" in one line of Marcellus near 6rem across the full width; under it, the lede and a solid white "Call (905) 276-2732" button with "Get a Quote" as a text link on the left; on the right, Elyia's review line set as the proof, with her name.

FORM: Quiet Luxury Workshop, the user's pick from three directions offered on 2026-10-27 (ranked second of three by me). seed: none (unavailable). The direction was pinned by the user, so no concept roll ran.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
